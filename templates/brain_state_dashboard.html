About the project

This dashboard visualizes EEG brain-wave signals and estimates cognitive states such as focus, relaxation, and stress. It supports research on brain waves and frequencies and combines lightweight machine learning with clear visualizations to show how mental state changes over time. Data shown here is sample data; connect your EMOTIV headset or data source to view live signals.
